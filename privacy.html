Privacy Policy

BumbleJohnGames · Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BumbleJohnGames ("we", "us", "our") handles information in our mobile games (the "Games"). It applies to all games published by BumbleJohnGames, including on Google Play. Our Games are made for players of all ages, including children, and we designed them to collect as little information as possible.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email address, phone number, or address. Our Games have no in-app purchases or payments. We do not collect personal information ourselves, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Our Games use Google AdMob to show ads, which may process limited device information as described in Section 2.

Game progress and settings (such as your score or volume) are stored locally on your device and are not sent to us.

2. Advertising

Our Games show ads from Google AdMob. Because our Games are made for all ages, including children, we configure AdMob to treat all users as children. Ads are non-personalized and limited to family-appropriate content, and we do not use your advertising ID for ad personalization.

To deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, AdMob may still process limited technical information, such as IP address, device type, operating system, and app interaction or diagnostic data. Google handles this information under its own policies: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy. We only use ad services certified for family apps.

3. Children's privacy

Our Games are designed to be appropriate for children. We design our Games to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy. In particular:

  • We treat all users of our Games as children for advertising purposes. Ads are requested with child-directed treatment enabled, which means personalized (interest-based) advertising is turned off and ads are limited to non-personalized, age-appropriate content.
  • We only use advertising services that are certified for use in apps for children.
  • We do not knowingly collect, use, or share personal information from children, and we do not include links to outside websites or social media in the Games, other than ads served by our certified ad provider.
  • We do not use any information from children for behavioral advertising or profiling.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the email below and we will work to delete it promptly.

4. Permissions

Our Games may use an internet connection so that ads can load. We do not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or precise location.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information, and we do not collect it ourselves. Limited technical information processed by AdMob is described in Section 2. We do not share information with any other third parties.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because we do not collect personal information, we have no personal data to retain or delete on our servers. Local game data on your device can be removed at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the Game. You can reset your device's advertising ID in your device settings (on Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads) at any time.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the Games and keep them free of unnecessary data collection. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, but collecting very little data helps limit risk.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, in the EU, UK, or California), you may have rights over your personal information, including access, correction, and deletion. Since we do not collect personal information ourselves, there is generally nothing for us to provide or delete, but you are welcome to contact us with any request or question. We do not request consent for personalized advertising because our Games do not show personalized ads. If you are in the EEA or UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date above. Please check back occasionally.

10. Contact us

Questions or concerns about this policy or our Games? Email us at bumblejohngames@gmail.com.
